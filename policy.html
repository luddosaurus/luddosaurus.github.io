Privacy Policy

What information do you collect?
> The app itself collects nothing. Everything is stored locally on your device. *Note: If you install the app via an app store (like Google Play), that store provides me with standard, anonymized analytics (such as total installs and active users per month) in accordance with their own privacy policies.*

How do you use the collected information?
> I don't

What collected information do you share?
> Nothing

How do I delete my data?
> Go to Appinfo -> Storage -> Clear data
> You can find the App info page in Setting/Apps or by long pressing the app icon (usually)

EU/EEA Users (GDPR)
> Because this application does not collect, transmit, or process any personal data off your device, it inherently complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). All data remains under your exclusive control.

How do I report bugs or suggest improvements?
> Because the app operates completely offline and has no telemetry or crash-reporting software, I have no way of knowing if the app crashes or if you encounter a bug. If you want to help improve the app, find an issue, or just have thoughts to share, you need to contact me directly.
